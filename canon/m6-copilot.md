# M6 — Teacher Copilot

M6 turns M5 decisions into minimal-disruption teacher assistance. It consumes `InterventionDecision + TeachingState + LessonMemory + TeacherPreferences + AttentionState` and returns assistance plus teacher action/outcome.

Modes: LIVE, QUIET, FOCUS, REVIEW, PAUSED, EMERGENCY. Assistance types: SIGNAL, PROMPT, VERIFICATION, CLARIFICATION, RECOMMENDATION, REMINDER, SUMMARY, EMERGENCY. Presentation must never be more intrusive than M5 authorizes.

The copilot remembers structured lesson state rather than retaining raw transcript by default. Teacher actions include acknowledge, accept, dismiss, defer, modify, verify, clarify, ask. Acknowledged does not mean accepted. Teacher correction becomes structured feedback. Personalization changes presentation, never constitutional policy gates. M6 cannot bypass M5.

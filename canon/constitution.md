# M0 — Constitution

TSA exists to represent, observe, reason about, and improve changing instructional states while preserving truth, teacher agency, student welfare, privacy, and instructional continuity.

## Non-negotiables
- State is primary; transcript is evidence.
- Observation, inference, evaluation, intervention, delivery, and outcome remain distinct.
- Uncertainty is first-class and consequential findings require traceable evidence.
- Teacher remains the primary human decision-maker; override is valid.
- Interventions use the lowest effective disruption level: L0 Observe, L1 Record, L2 Signal, L3 Prompt, L4 Interrupt.
- M5 decisions are deterministic and policy-driven.
- Confidence below 0.80 cannot produce L3/L4.
- L4 requires critical severity, confidence >=0.90, immediate welfare/knowledge-integrity risk, and low disruption cost.
- Repeated findings (>=3) and resolved findings are suppressed.
- Raw audio is temporary processing input, not the permanent lesson representation.
- Student identity is not required for the core teacher-state engine.
- M7 cannot silently mutate production policy; improvements are versioned and validated.
- No single teacher-quality score is a primitive.

## Constitutional test
Does a component improve understanding or instructional state without unnecessarily compromising truth, agency, welfare, privacy, or continuity?
